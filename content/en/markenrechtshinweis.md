## Please contact us before issuing a cease-and-desist

If you believe that this website or the ForgeIEC project
**makes unauthorised use of trademark, copyright or personality
rights**, we explicitly ask you to send **a brief informal email**
to:

**[blacksmith@forgeiec.io](mailto:blacksmith@forgeiec.io)**

Because the project **generates no revenue** and pursues no
commercial interests, an immediate **fee-incurring legal
cease-and-desist** would be **disproportionate** under the
established case law of DACH jurisdictions (see, among others,
BGH, judgment of 12 December 2013, I ZR 131/12). The project
maintainer takes responsibility seriously and will respond to
**any substantiated complaint without delay**:

- the disputed content will typically be removed or changed
  **within 48 hours**,
- a **binding declaration to cease and desist** will be issued
  voluntarily where an infringement actually exists,
- the costs of an initial legal letter will be **rejected as
  unfounded** because, absent prior direct contact, such costs
  are neither necessary nor proportionate.

We therefore ask for understanding that we **will reject legal
fee notes that are issued without prior direct contact** as a
matter of principle.
