# ForgeIEC Contributor License Agreement

{{< callout type="warning" title="Draft — legal review required" >}}
This text is a proposal. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer, it is
**not in force** and it is not binding. **Until the CLA is in force, no
third-party contributions are accepted.** Bug reports and feature
requests as issues are welcome.
{{< /callout >}}

{{< callout type="note" title="What this means for you" >}}
- **Who needs to sign:** everyone who submits a contribution (source
  code, documentation, graphics, data) to a ForgeIEC repository — once
  per contributor, not per pull request. If you write the contribution
  as an employee, you need your employer's written permission.
  Reporting a bug or asking for a feature in an issue needs no CLA.
- **What you keep:** you stay the author of your contribution and are
  named as such. The rights you grant are non-exclusive: you may keep
  using your own contribution in any other way.
- **What the author may do:** Jörg Bernau may use, change and
  distribute your contribution and license it under any licence —
  AGPL-3.0-or-later, Apache-2.0 or a commercial licence. This is what
  keeps ForgeIEC available under both the open-source licences and a
  commercial licence. He is not obliged to use your contribution.
{{< /callout >}}

## Why a CLA

ForgeIEC is developed and maintained by Jörg Bernau (alias
"Blacksmith"), who alone holds the copyright. The platform core is
offered under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**; everything that is linked into the
customer's PLC project (the vendor-facing runtime API, the runtime
library and the generated code) is under **Apache-2.0**. In addition,
the author offers a **commercial licence**. That is only possible while
the author holds all rights in the code. Contributions therefore need
this agreement, which gives the author the right to relicense them.

This one text applies to all ForgeIEC repositories; each repository
points here from its `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## The agreement

1. **Parties.** "You" is the person (or the legal entity on whose behalf
   that person acts) who submits a contribution. "The Author" is Jörg
   Bernau, the copyright holder of ForgeIEC, and his legal successors.

2. **Contribution.** A contribution is any work (source code,
   documentation, graphics, data) that You submit to a ForgeIEC
   repository, for example as a pull request, patch or issue attachment.

3. **Grant of rights.** You grant the Author a non-exclusive, worldwide,
   perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free and transferable right to use
   Your contribution in all known and future types of use, in
   particular to reproduce, distribute, make publicly available, modify
   and combine it with other works.

4. **Right to relicense.** The grant includes the right to license Your
   contribution, alone or as part of ForgeIEC, to third parties under
   any licence of the Author's choice — including AGPL-3.0-or-later,
   Apache-2.0 and proprietary/commercial licences — and to grant
   sub-licences.

5. **Patents.** You grant the Author and every recipient of ForgeIEC a
   perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence for
   claims that You can license and that are necessarily infringed by
   Your contribution alone or by its combination with ForgeIEC.

6. **Authorship.** Your authorship is not affected (§ 13 UrhG). You keep
   the right to use Your own contribution in any other way.

7. **Your assurances.** You confirm that the contribution is Your own
   original work, or that You have the right to grant the rights above
   (in particular if it was created in an employment relationship,
   § 69b UrhG — then with the employer's written permission), and that
   it does not contain code from a source whose licence conflicts with
   this agreement.

8. **No obligation.** The Author is not obliged to use Your contribution.

9. **Form.** The CLA is accepted once per contributor, in the form the
   Author will publish together with the final text (for example a
   signed document or a confirmation in the pull request).

10. **Applicable law.** German law applies.

**Still open:** whether a grant of rights (as above) or a transfer model
is used.
