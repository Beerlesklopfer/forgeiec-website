# Contact

Write to us with the form on this page or by e-mail. Every message is read by a person.

## What you can write to us about

- **Request a pilot** — you would like to try ForgeIEC on a machine or plant? Briefly describe the control task, the target hardware and field buses, and your time frame.
- **Licences** — questions about the licence terms, commercial use or embedding ForgeIEC in your own products.
- **Partnership / contributing** — for integrators, hardware vendors and developers who want to contribute. How working together works is described on the [Partners](/en/partner/) page.
- **Job / collaboration** — for companies and recruiters who would like to work with the developer of ForgeIEC.
- **Other** — anything else, including errors on this website.
- **E-mail instead of the form:** [blacksmith@forgeiec.io](mailto:blacksmith@forgeiec.io)

{{< callout type="note" title="Good to know" >}}
We answer as soon as we can, but we cannot promise a particular response time.
Please do not report security vulnerabilities through this form; follow the
[disclosure policy](/security/disclosure-policy/) instead.
{{< /callout >}}
