## In short

This website sets **no cookies**, uses **no tracking and no analytics** and loads **no third-party content**: no fonts from other servers, no embedded videos or maps, no scripts from content delivery networks. All files – fonts (your device's system fonts), scripts and the diagram library – come from forgeiec.io itself.

## Hosting and server logs

The website runs on its own server ([Owner to provide: hosting provider or server location]). For every request the web server (nginx) processes technically necessary data and writes it to an access log:

- IP address of the requesting device
- date and time of the request
- requested address (URL) and status code
- browser identifier (user agent) and, if sent, the referring page

The purpose is secure and stable operation (troubleshooting, defence against attacks). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Retention period: [Owner to provide]. The data is not combined with other data.

## Cookies and local storage {#cookies}

<!-- ENTWURF: legal review needed: TDDDG §25 and GDPR Art. 6 -->

**Cookies:** this website sets no cookies.

**Local storage in the browser (localStorage):** the website stores only your own settings, and only when you change them yourself. The values stay in your browser and are not sent to the server:

| Key | Content | When it is set |
|---|---|---|
| `forgeiec-theme` | `light` or `dark` – the chosen appearance | when you use the light/dark toggle; without your choice the site follows your operating system's colour scheme and stores nothing |
| `forgeiec-sound` | `on` or `off` – whether the forge sound plays | when you use the forge sound toggle |

You can delete these entries at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data"). If storage is blocked, the site still works; the setting then lasts only until you leave the page.

**No consent banner:** because the website stores only what is strictly necessary for a function you explicitly requested, in our assessment no consent under § 25 (1) TDDDG is required (exception under § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). As soon as analytics, third-party embeds or fonts from other servers are added, a consent banner becomes necessary and this policy will be updated.
