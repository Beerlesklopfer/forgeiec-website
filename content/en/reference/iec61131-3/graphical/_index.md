## Graphical languages

The two graph-based PLC languages of IEC 61131-3:

### [Ladder Diagram (LD)](ladder-diagram/)

Relay-ladder notation: rungs of contacts and coils between two
power rails. Closest match to traditional electrical schematics
and the on-ramp for electricians who are new to programming. The
ForgeIEC editor exposes LD via a graph editor; the persisted
form is PLCopen XML. (Stub.)

### [Function Block Diagram (FBD)](function-block-diagram/)

Boxes-and-wires notation: function blocks are blocks, signals
are wires. Closest match to control-loop diagrams. Editor
exposes FBD via the same graph editor as LD; persistence is
PLCopen XML. (Stub.)
